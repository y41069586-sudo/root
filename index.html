FAQ

Before you download

The questions people ask us most.

How accurate is the skin score?

The score is a consistent reading, not a medical measurement. Glowé looks at what a front camera can actually see — surface texture, tone, redness and visible blemishes — under reasonable light. Its real strength is comparability: the same scale every time, so day 1 and day 14 line up. It cannot see under the skin and it does not diagnose anything.

What does Glowé cost?

Downloading, your first scan and your full skin score are free. Glowé Plus — unlimited re-scans, the full 14-day routine and progress history — is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID. The exact price for your region is shown in the App Store and on the purchase screen before you confirm. You can cancel any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What happens to my photos?

They are processed to produce your analysis and stored so you can compare scans over time. They are never sold and never passed to advertising networks. You can delete individual scans or your entire account, photos included, from the app — see our privacy policy for the full detail.

Do I need to buy specific products?

No. Glowé plans around categories — cleanser, serum, moisturiser, SPF — and you can add the products you already own. We do not sell products and we take no commission on any we mention.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 16.0 or later. An Android version is on the roadmap; there is no release date yet, so please don't plan around one.

Something's broken. Who do I talk to?

A real person, usually within one working day. Head to Support or write straight to support@glowe.app — a screenshot helps enormously.
